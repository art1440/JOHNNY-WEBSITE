# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (inherited from the main-branch site). Hosted as static files.

## Users
Hispanic families in the US shopping for life insurance, many arriving on a phone from a social ad. Spanish-first; some prefer English. Inferred: wide age range (young parents through 65+ buying final-expense coverage), often wary of scams and faceless call centers. They need someone they trust and who seems legitimate.

## Product Purpose
A landing page for an independent, licensed life insurance agent. The visitor answers a short auto-advancing quiz (the owner's favorite element) and leaves contact details; the agent follows up by WhatsApp, call or text. Success is a submitted lead from a visitor who feels they are dealing with a real, licensed person.

## Positioning
A real, named, licensed agent who speaks the family's language, not a lead-gen call center. Personal contact (WhatsApp, direct number), verifiable license.

## Operating Context
Visitors come from ads on mobile, read in Spanish, and contact through WhatsApp more than email. Leads go to a CRM via webhook (config.js).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Six-question auto-advancing quiz, then name/phone/ZIP/optional email; consent text for calls, texts and WhatsApp.
- Bilingual ES/EN toggle; Spanish is the default.
- Template: agent name, photo, license, NPN, states, phone, WhatsApp, testimonials and carrier logos are pending from the owner.

## Brand Commitments
- Owner feedback on v1: it looked AI-made. It must look unique, warm and welcoming.
- Keep the auto-advancing "pop-up" questions.

## Evidence on Hand
None yet. Testimonials are samples and must stay labeled as samples until real ones arrive. No ratings, customer counts or carrier names may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. A real person, visibly: face, name, license number, direct line.
2. Native Spanish, not translated Spanish; usted form, plain words.
3. Calm, never pushy: no countdowns, fake urgency or fake social-proof pop-ups.
4. Warmth that still reads as legitimate business.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Older visitors: large body type, high contrast, generous tap targets; reduced-motion support.
